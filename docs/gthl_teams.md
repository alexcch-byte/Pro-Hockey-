# GTHL clubs (Power Play Hockey, league GTHL)

Club names from the GTHL members feed (https://gthlcanada.com/wp-json/wp/v2/members, category competitive). Colours are approximations researched from public sources; clubs' own sites expose no colour data. No logos are used: crests are drawn procedurally from colours + abbreviation. Every club has a generic white road kit (`awayKit`); `TeamInfo.matchTeams` picks road kits when jerseys clash.
