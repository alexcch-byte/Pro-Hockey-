# Graphics reference (target look)

The user supplied four screenshots of a polished mobile hockey game ("Arcade Hockey"/"Hockey All-Stars" style)
as the ideal graphics goal. The critic judges the Android build against this description.

- Camera: broadcast-style perspective, ~30-40 degrees elevation, following the play; rink fills the screen,
  far boards/net visible at top, near boards cut off at the bottom. Side lines converge toward the far end.
- Ice: bright white, subtle scuffs/reflections; crisp red centre line with dashes, blue lines, faceoff circles
  and dots, thin goal line, red crease; large centre-ice logo; team-coloured faceoff circle fill at faceoff.
- Boards/glass: dark-topped boards with sponsor panels along the whole wall, glass above them with highlights;
  stands full of a dense, colourful crowd (red/orange/yellow shirts) behind, with ad strips.
- Players: chunky, readable 3D-style skaters with helmets, jerseys in team colours, visible numbers on the back,
  sticks held in hands, pants/socks/skates; shadows on the ice; distinct goalie with pads and mask;
  referee in black-and-white stripes. Controlled player has a bright GREEN ring under the skates.
- HUD: slim top bar, centred: team abbreviations + logo crests, scores, period ("1st") and clock; "PULL GOALIE"
  and pause buttons top right. Faceoff/period overlay: bottom banner with big team crests, names, score.
- Controls: translucent circular buttons bottom right: SHOOT (crosshair icon), PASS (puck-and-arrow icon),
  (the reference also has DEKE; this project removed it - deke is triggered by rapid joystick flicks).
  Joystick bottom left: translucent ring with a thumb knob.
- Overall: clean, bright, saturated colours, strong contrast, no clutter, smooth 60 fps.
